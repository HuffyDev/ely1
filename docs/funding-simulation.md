# Funding mechanism — simulation results

Generated by `npm run sim` in `keeper/` (seeded, reproducible; 4,000 rounds per cell). Exact bigint contract math (`src/payout.ts`, which is fuzz-tested to match the Solidity) is used for every payout.

**Agent model.** Each round, N agents arrive one at a time with a lognormal stake (≈0.6 HYPE median, max 10).
- *Noise traders* pick short with the stated bias regardless of price.
- *EV traders* estimate P(down) with ±3% noise, look at the live pool (including funding and the payout cap), and join whichever side has positive expected value, otherwise abstain.

Positive "ROI" means that group's average return per unit staked. Fees are 2% of value transferred.

## Key finding (read before approving)

**In this model, funding barely changes skew.** Mean and p90 skew are almost identical from k=0 to k=2000. The parimutuel odds already make the minority side +EV, so EV traders join it whether or not there is funding. What funding mainly changes is *who earns what*: it moves value from biased short-spammers to balancing traders, roughly proportional to k. In thin rounds it also roughly doubles the guaranteed edge for anyone willing to take the empty side. That is the behaviour you want in order to bootstrap market makers, but it is a real cost to retail.

Skew is mainly limited by **how much balancing capital shows up**, not by funding. Practical recommendations:
1. Keep a modest default (k=1000, max 5%, capped at 50% of minority stake) and show it prominently in the UI as "shorts pay longs x%".
2. Show live implied odds, because that is what actually rebalances.
3. Accept that thin rounds will be lopsided, or one-sided and refunded (12.6% of rounds in the thin scenario).
4. Don't raise k thinking it fixes skew. Raising it only increases the transfer.

## How to read this

- The pari-mutuel pool already rebalances on its own: if 80% of the money is short, a winning long earns about 4x. Funding adds a payment to the minority side that does not depend on the outcome. That draws in balancing capital sooner and makes lopsided rounds cheaper to fix.
- Funding is a zero-sum transfer from the crowded side to the minority side. It never creates liabilities, and the conservation fuzz tests prove the totals always match.
- The funding cap (50% of minority stake) keeps a 1-wei minority deposit from collecting a large guaranteed payment.

**Status: proposed, not final.** The parameters are owner-settable within hard bounds, and each round snapshots them on its first deposit. Review these tables before mainnet.
