## 1. What SLOPX is

SLOPX is an interface to an experimental, unaudited smart contract on the Elysium network. The contract runs pari-mutuel pools on the hourly return of an index of newly launched tokens. It does not offer investment advice, custody or brokerage, and it is not a regulated exchange.

## 2. Eligibility and restricted regions

You must not use SLOPX if:

- you are under 18, or under the age of majority where you live;
- you are located in, or a resident or citizen of, a **restricted region**, which currently includes the United States, United Kingdom, France, Singapore, Australia, and any country under comprehensive sanctions (including North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, Russia and Belarus);
- using a product like this is illegal where you live. Products like this may count as **gambling, betting, binary options or derivatives**, and many places restrict or ban them.

You are responsible for knowing and following your local law. Using a VPN to get around restrictions breaches these terms.

## 3. Risks you accept

- **Total loss.** If your side loses, you lose your deposit, apart from any cap refund.
- **Smart contract risk.** The code is unaudited and may have bugs that lose funds.
- **Oracle / keeper risk.** An operator-run keeper posts results. A wrong result can be posted, and the dispute protections may fail. See the [trust model](#/docs/trust).
- **Data risk.** Index prices come from third-party sources and thin markets that can be wrong or manipulated. See the [threat model](#/docs/threats).
- **Market manipulation.** Others may try to move constituent prices.
- **Network risk.** Elysium is a new chain. Outages, reorgs or bugs in it can affect rounds.
- **Wallet risk.** You are responsible for your own keys. No one can reverse a transaction.
- **Regulatory risk.** Access may be restricted or the service shut down at any time.
- **Tax.** Any taxes are your responsibility.

## 4. No guarantees

SLOPX is provided **as is**, with no warranty of any kind. The operator is not liable for any loss, to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 5. Fees

A fee (currently 2%) is taken from value moving from the losing side to the winning side. The fee goes to the treasury. Refunded rounds pay no fee.

## 6. Changes

These terms may change. Parameters for a round in progress cannot change after its first deposit.
